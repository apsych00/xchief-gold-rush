Decisions (do not redesign):
1. Share token: `players.share_token text unique` filled on first share with 12 random base64url chars (`gen_random_bytes(9)`), never rotated. The share URL is `<PUBLIC_URL>/s/<token>`. A frame `{type:'share_link'}` (player sockets only, 1/s budget) returns `{type:'share_link', url, record, rank, tier, display}`; anonymous players get a token too (their masked display is "Guest").
2. Modal (web only, from the profile's Share button, replacing the bare `navigator.share`): reuses `.modal-backdrop .modal`. Contents: a banner preview card (the gift-card styles: xChief mark, "My Gold Rush record", the record in coins, rank and tier badge when ranked, the tournament title), the share URL in a read-only field with a Copy button (clipboard API, toast "Link copied"), and four shortcut buttons: Telegram (`https://t.me/share/url?url=<url>&text=<text>`), WhatsApp (`https://wa.me/?text=<text url>`), X (`https://twitter.com/intent/tweet?text=<text>&url=<url>`), and "More" which calls `navigator.share` when available and is hidden otherwise. Instagram has no share URL: not listed. Share text is the existing `profile.shareText` with the record and the new URL.
3. Public share page at `/s/<token>`: routed like the claim page. `GET /api/share/<token>` (public, no auth, under S2's per-IP window) returns `{display, record, rank, tier, tournament_title}` or 404; nothing else about the player is exposed. The page shows the same banner card, then two CTAs: "Play Gold Rush" (link to `/`) and "Join the tournament" (link to `/` as well, with the tournament title in the button when one is running). Unknown token: the card says "This record is not available" and the CTAs still show. The page carries proper `<title>` and Open Graph tags (title, description with the record, `og:image` pointing at a static `public/share/og.png` placeholder in the brand colours, 1200x630) so pasted links preview.
4. Kiosk never shows share.
